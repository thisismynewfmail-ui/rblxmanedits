How to play

Chat

Chat sits in the lower left. While the chat box is open every other key is ignored, so you can type freely. Double click a username in chat to open that player's profile in a new tab.

Unusual items

Buying a hat from the market has a 0.5% chance of producing an Unusual copy. Unusual hats carry a particle effect that renders on your head in every world and on every avatar preview across the site. Only hats can roll Unusual.

Visits

A visit is recorded once you have been in a world for 30 seconds.

Instances

Every world has a per-round capacity. When an instance fills up the host opens a new one, so the browser might show "2 instances, 30 players" for a world whose round size is 24.

Worlds

Burger Tycoon — claim a plot, buy machines, collect coins, build up your restaurant. Endless; no teams shuffle.

Capture The Flag — first to three captures wins the round, then everyone votes on shuffling the teams.

Fortress Team 2 — push the bomb cart to the enemy base. Teams swap ends every round; first to three round wins takes the match.
